Our story

Started because commercial sites don't leave room for finish work

Dave Ferrow spent twelve years doing shopfitting joinery before starting on his own in 2009 — tired of being told to rush finish carpentry to keep a commercial job moving. Since 2014 the workshop's been based on the Springwood industrial estate in Braintree, where every fitted piece is measured, cut and built before it ever reaches your house.

No sales team, no subcontracted fitters — the person who measures your room is the person who builds and fits the work.
