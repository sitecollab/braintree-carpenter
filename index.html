About

Seventeen years, one workshop, no subcontractors

Dave Ferrow started the business in 2009 after twelve years with a shopfitting firm in Chelmsford, tired of being told to rush finish carpentry on commercial sites. Everything is built off-site at the Springwood workshop in Braintree and fitted by the person who measured it — no handoff, no surprises on the day.
